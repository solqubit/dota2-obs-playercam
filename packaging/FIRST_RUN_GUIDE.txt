OverPower Dota Player Cams  -  First-run guide
================================================

What it does: when you select a player in Dota 2 (spectator mode), the app
turns on that player's camera in OBS (and turns off the previous one). It
works on its own; just keep it open.

You need: Windows 10/11, Dota 2 and OBS Studio installed, and an OBS scene:
the one your event organizer sent you, or the example included in this .zip
("example-scene.json"). Nothing else to install: the app brings everything
it needs.


1) INSTALL
   - Extract the WHOLE .zip (right click -> Extract All). Do not run the app
     from inside the zip and do not move the .exe out of its folder: it needs
     the files next to it.
   - Open "DotaPlayerCams.exe" inside the DotaPlayerCams folder.
   - Language: the app starts in your Windows language (Spanish or English).
     Change it in Settings -> Language; it stays fixed.

2) IF WINDOWS SAYS "WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC" (SmartScreen)
   The app has no paid digital signature, so Windows does not recognize the
   publisher. It does not mean it is dangerous.
   - Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It only shows up the first time.

3) IF YOUR ANTIVIRUS BLOCKS OR DELETES IT
   The app listens to keys and clicks while Dota is the active window (that is
   how it switches cameras instantly), and some antivirus tools mistake that
   for spyware.
   - Windows Security -> Virus & threat protection -> Manage settings ->
     Exclusions -> Add or remove exclusions -> Add an exclusion -> Folder.
   - Choose the whole DotaPlayerCams folder and open the app again.
   - If it was deleted, restore it from the antivirus protection history,
     then add the exclusion.

4) IMPORT THE SCENE INTO OBS
   - In OBS: "Scene Collection" menu -> "Import". Add the scene .json file
     and confirm.
   - Then "Scene Collection" -> pick the imported collection (the example is
     called "Dota Player Cams (example)").
   - With the example: in each group CAM_EQ1_1 ... CAM_EQ2_5, open the VDO_...
     source and replace its URL with that player's VDO.Ninja link.

5) TURN ON OBS WEBSOCKET AND ENTER THE PASSWORD
   - In OBS: Tools -> "WebSocket Server Settings" -> tick "Enable WebSocket
     server". Copy the password (or create one).
   - In the app: "Settings" button (top right) -> paste it into "OBS
     WebSocket password" -> "Save". It is stored only on this PC.

6) FOLLOW THE CHECKS ON SCREEN
   On start, the app checks OBS, Dota, the scene and the hotkeys. On the left
   each item is green (good), yellow (warning) or red (missing), with
   instructions or a "Fix" button. The app never changes anything without
   asking, and Dota hotkeys are only fixed with Dota closed.
   Click "Re-check everything" after each fix until everything is green.
   Border and frame images: put them as Borde.png and Marco.png in the folder
   DotaPlayerCams\_internal\assets and click "Fix".

7) DURING THE MATCH ("Live" panel, on the right)
   - "Team 1 side": choose Radiant or Dire after the coin toss.
   - "Mode": Automatic = the camera follows the player you select.
     "Hide all" = no camera on air (the app stops listening to keyboard and
     mouse).
   Keep the window open while you stream.

Something not working? Look first at which item is red or yellow in the
Checks panel; it almost always tells you what to do.
